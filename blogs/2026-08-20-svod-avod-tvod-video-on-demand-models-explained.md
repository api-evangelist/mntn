Open a streaming app today, and you’re not just choosing what to watch, but also how to pay for it. Either with a monthly subscription, a few ads, a one-time fee, or some hybrid combination of the three. That decision shapes everything from the price viewers pay for content to the opportunities advertisers have to reach them.
